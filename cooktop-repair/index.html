Common JennAir Cooktop Problems

Burner Not Heating

Electric and induction elements can fail from bad generators, switches, coils, or damaged control components.

Igniter Clicking but No Flame

Gas cooktops often need burner cleaning, spark diagnosis, switch repair, or gas-flow troubleshooting.

Cracked Glass Surface

We inspect the glass top, mounting, and affected components to determine whether safe replacement is needed.

Touch Controls Not Responding

Failed user interfaces, moisture intrusion, and board communication faults can disable JennAir cooktop controls.

Intermittent Power or Shutoff

Loose connections, failing modules, overheating protection, or supply issues can cause unreliable operation.

JennAir Cooktop FAQ

Why is my JennAir cooktop burner not working?

The cause may be a bad switch, failed element, weak spark, damaged module, or a power-supply issue.

Do you repair JennAir induction cooktops?

Yes. We service gas, radiant electric, and induction JennAir cooktops used in homes across DFW.

Can a cracked cooktop glass be repaired?

Glass itself is typically replaced rather than repaired. We inspect the unit and advise on the correct replacement path.

Is same-day cooktop service available?

Yes. Same-day diagnostics are available in many DFW areas for ignition, burner, and control problems.
