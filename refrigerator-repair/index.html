JennAir Refrigerator Problems We Fix

Not Cooling

Cooling complaints often come from evaporator fan, thermistor, defrost, damper, or sealed-system faults.

Ice Maker Not Working

We repair fill tube freeze-ups, inlet valve failures, ice maker assemblies, and temperature-related ice production issues.

Water Leaking

Blocked drains, filter head leaks, and water line issues commonly cause puddles under JennAir refrigerators.

Fresh Food Warm, Freezer Cold

Airflow restrictions, damper faults, and evaporator fan problems can leave one section warm while the freezer stays cold.

Loud Fan or Compressor Noise

Rattling, buzzing, and constant running usually point to fan motors, start components, or cooling-system trouble.
