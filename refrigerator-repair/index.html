Common JennAir Refrigerator Problems

Not Cooling

We diagnose fan motors, defrost faults, thermistors, dampers, sealed-system issues, and control failures.

Ice Maker Not Working

Common causes include fill tube freeze-ups, inlet valve failures, low temperature faults, and bad ice maker assemblies.

Water Leaking

Drain clogs, water line issues, cracked fittings, and filter head leaks can leave water under or inside the refrigerator.

Freezer Cold, Fresh Food Warm

Airflow restrictions, evaporator fan problems, and damper or defrost issues often cause uneven compartment temperatures.

Loud Noise or Constant Running

Buzzing, rattling, or nonstop operation usually points to fan, compressor start, condenser, or airflow problems.

JennAir Refrigerator FAQ

Why is my JennAir refrigerator not cooling?

The most common causes are evaporator fan issues, defrost failures, sensor problems, dirty condenser coils, or a sealed-system fault.
