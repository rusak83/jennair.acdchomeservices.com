JennAir Oven Problems We Fix

Not Heating

Failed bake or broil circuits, igniters, sensors, and control boards can all stop a JennAir oven from heating.

Uneven Baking

Temperature drift, convection fan issues, and bad sensors often cause undercooked centers or hot spots.

Error Codes

We diagnose JennAir oven faults tied to probes, latches, sensors, and main control communication failures.

Door Won't Close or Lock

Hinge wear, latch motor problems, and gasket damage can prevent safe oven operation and self-clean cycles.

Display or Touch Panel Issues

Dead controls, partial response, and electronic glitches often point to failed UI boards or power problems.
