Common JennAir Oven Problems

Not Heating

Failed bake or broil elements, igniters, relays, sensors, and control boards can all cause no-heat complaints.

Uneven Baking

Temperature sensor drift, convection fan issues, calibration problems, and door gasket leaks affect consistency.

Error Codes on Display

JennAir ovens may show fault codes tied to probes, sensors, latch systems, UI panels, or main control failures.

Door Won't Close or Lock

Broken hinges, latch motors, alignment problems, and worn seals can prevent normal cooking or self-clean operation.

Control Panel Not Responding

Touch panel failures, communication faults, and damaged boards can leave the oven partially or fully inoperable.

JennAir Oven FAQ

Why is my JennAir oven not heating?

Typical causes include a failed igniter, bad bake element, faulty temperature sensor, relay failure, or control board issue.

Do you repair JennAir double wall ovens?

Yes. We service single and double JennAir wall ovens, including built-in configurations common in DFW homes.

Can uneven baking be fixed?

Usually yes. We test sensor accuracy, airflow, convection performance, and calibration before recommending parts.
