Common JennAir Range Problems

Burner Not Igniting

Igniters, spark modules, switches, burner heads, and gas flow issues commonly stop JennAir burners from lighting.

Oven Not Heating

Range ovens may fail from bad igniters, bake elements, relays, temperature sensors, or control board issues.

Uneven Temperature

Calibration drift, convection faults, weak igniters, or sensor problems can cause undercooking or hot spots.

Error Codes or Dead Display

UI boards, power issues, communication faults, and failed controls can shut down normal range operation.

Gas Smell or Safety Concern

We diagnose ignition delay, valve problems, burner issues, and unsafe operation that needs immediate attention.

JennAir Range FAQ

Why is my JennAir range burner clicking but not lighting?

That usually points to burner alignment, moisture, a dirty burner head, a weak spark, or gas-flow issues.

Do you repair dual-fuel JennAir ranges?

Yes. We service gas, electric, and dual-fuel JennAir ranges, including pro-style and slide-in models.

Can you fix oven temperature problems on a range?

Yes. We test ignition, sensor accuracy, convection performance, and board control to restore stable cooking temperatures.

Should I call right away if I smell gas?

Yes. Stop using the appliance and schedule service promptly if you suspect an ignition or gas-delivery problem.
